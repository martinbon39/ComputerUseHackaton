\section{Value-axis construction}
\label{app:value}
The value direction of \texttt{astra-value} contrasts success with failure steps of the same relative stage, averaged within task, on 68 construction tasks; the commitment direction contrasts \texttt{answer} with continuation steps. Neither uses the \fsp/\fsm{} labels. Evaluated on \fsp{} versus \fsm{} under task-grouped cross-validation, the value direction scores below 0.5 at layers 18, 24 and 28, so it is higher on true completions, while step index and trajectory length reach about 0.85. % source: data/pod_astra/value/REPORT.md "Lecture exploratoire en CV par tâche"
Its separation is thus weaker than position, the same confound as Trap~1.
