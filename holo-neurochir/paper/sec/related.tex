\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Directions, steering and ablation.} Behaviour-specific directions are typically difference-of-means vectors added at inference \citep{turner2023actadd,rimsky2023caa,zou2023repe,li2023iti} or projected out \citep{arditi2024refusal}; linear structure has also been reported for truthfulness \citep{marks2023geometry}. Refusal in chat models is mediated by a single direction, and ablating it everywhere equals orthogonalising every writer \citep{arditi2024refusal}, the equivalence of \S\ref{sec:compile}. Venhoff et al.\ steer backtracking and uncertainty in thinking models \citep{venhoff2025reasoning}. Weight-space programming removes a looping habit by editing \texttt{down\_proj} columns \citep{takehi2026wsp}.

\paragraph{Agents, tools and termination.} Tool identity is linearly readable and steerable, with weaker effects in multi-turn settings \citep{wu2026toolcalling}. A direction can set the rate of tool calls \citep{chen2026toolrate}, agents encode when to call a tool before reasoning \citep{sun2026when2tool}, and steering has served as procedural memory for agents \citep{zhao2026npm}. Huang et al.\ study when GUI agents should not act \citep{huang2026conflict}. Models carry a value axis for being on the right track \citep{jiang2026value}; confidence tracks commitment \citep{kumaran2026commit} and supports self-detection of errors \citep{kumaran2026selfdetect}; overconfidence has a mechanistic account \citep{overconfidence2026}; and the decision to stop thinking runs through late MLPs \citep{dutta2026termination}.

\paragraph{Pitfalls.} A probe shows decodability, not use \citep{belinkov2021probing}; control tasks \citep{hewitt2019control} and confound residualisation \citep{sahoo2026format} are needed. Steering vectors generalise unevenly \citep{tan2024steering,dasilva2025steering}, can compromise safety \citep{korznikov2025rogue}, push activations off the natural manifold \citep{mishra2026nonsurjective}, and are often beaten by prompting \citep{wu2025axbench}. Patching has its own best practices \citep{zhang2023patching,heimersheim2024patching,wang2022ioi}, and weight editing goes back to causal tracing \citep{meng2022rome}.
