\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Small causal samples.} Each 4B screen uses six contexts from five tasks, and the reasoning analysis uses four tasks. These are screens for a lever, not estimates of effect size. A small effect on other tasks could have been missed, and we report ``no admissible effect found'', not ``no effect exists''.
\item \textbf{The metric targets the wrong action on infeasible tasks.} \oswFsInfeasible{} of \oswCatFalseSuccess{} false successes are claims of completion on infeasible tasks, where \texttt{answer} with a refusal is the correct tool; one such context enters several screens and the ceiling test. Appendix~\ref{app:infeasible} gives every count with and without it.
\item \textbf{Many uncorrected comparisons.} Every arm of Tables~\ref{tab:causal}, \ref{tab:expo} and~\ref{tab:attractor}, the merges and the closed-form edits was screened on the same few contexts, and \onNArms{} arms were run online, without correction for multiple comparisons; no intervention on the model is claimed to work, and none would survive a correction. The one positive result, the guard's lower false ``done'' rate (\S\ref{sec:guard}), is the primary criterion of its own registration, from a single \textsc{test} run.
\item \textbf{The test split was used by \onNTestRuns{} online runs and the guarded agent} (\S\ref{sec:online}, \S\ref{sec:guard}): the original (served twice), ExPO, which had not passed the gate, a partial corrective-prompt run, and the CUA-Lite merge under a decision rule fixed in advance.
\item \textbf{The confirmation reserve was used outside the protocol} (\S\ref{sec:online}). The protocol kept it to confirm a committed candidate; no candidate was admitted, and five non-admitted arms were run on it: ExPO, the \texttt{answer} output-row edit, the corrective prompt, the CUA-Lite merge and the closed-form edit. It is no longer an untouched set for a future candidate.
\item \textbf{Self-registered rules, LLM auditors and an LLM coordinator.} Our registrations are timestamped entries in our own repository, not a public registry; the primary online rule was fixed after the merge's reserve scores had been read, so only its \textsc{test} part is strictly prospective. The semantic audits and ``independent'' reviews are LLM agents, not validated against human raters, and some protocol decisions (for instance ExPO on the \textsc{test} split) were taken by an LLM coordinator.
\item \textbf{One model, one benchmark.} Every result concerns Holo-3.1-4B (and the 0.8B) on OSWorld (and MiniWoB++) with one harness; the mechanistic analyses are exploratory, select and estimate on the same few contexts, and use artificial teacher-forced inputs. The Claude verifier may have seen public OSWorld tasks. The judge was called through the Claude CLI alias \texttt{sonnet} (Claude Sonnet, version not pinned); the Holo4-27B weights are \texttt{Hcompany/Holo4-27B} from Hugging Face at revision \texttt{4de03cfe}, as recorded in our deployment manifests. % source: data/local/modal_deploys/20261108T075938_acct2_Holo4-27B.json, 20261108T090300_acct2_Holo4-27B-agent.json (checkpoint Hcompany/Holo4-27B@4de03cfeee1d930b616bfd4817570293f29ad8b9)
\item \textbf{Exploratory direction analysis.} The official analysis passed the 40-positive gate, but the second pass stopped at \rtmPassTwoScored{} tasks, so it is labelled exploratory; the steering screens predate it.
\item \textbf{Automatic labels.} The 1\% screen-change threshold, the 15\,px click radius and the refusal regex are design choices, fixed before analysis but not validated against human labels.
\item \textbf{Harness.} 50 steps, three screenshots and a third-party public harness (RealtimeGUI), not H Company's, whose infeasibility rule is broader than the official OSWorld adapter's (\S\ref{sec:setting}); absolute scores are not comparable with published numbers, paired comparisons are. One correct refusal is scored 0 because the harness's refusal regex misses it, and we kept the official score. % source: JOURNAL.md "Limite de la regex du harness"
\item \textbf{One family of directions.} Difference-of-means, matched pairs, PCA, attribution-selected neurons and heads. Sparse autoencoders and multi-direction subspaces were not tested.
\item \textbf{Shared compute.} Research jobs shared one GPU with the rollout server and paused on a latency flag; interrupted runs are disclosed and resumed exactly, never counted as results.
\end{itemize}
